# Homepage Design Upgrade

## Goal

Make the app feel like a real product instead of only a dashboard.

## New User Flow

1. Home page explains the business problem
2. User uploads support ticket data or chooses demo data
3. App validates data quality
4. App runs SLA, sentiment, risk, workload, and root-cause analysis
5. AI agents generate ticket triage and daily briefing
6. User downloads manager report

## Homepage Sections

- Hero section
- Customer experience impact statistics
- What the app does
- AI workflow
- Target users

## Business Impact Stats Used

- PwC: 32% of customers would stop doing business with a brand they love after one bad experience.
- Zendesk: More than 50% of customers may switch after a single unsatisfactory customer experience.
- Qualtrics XM Institute: Poor customer experiences could put nearly $3 trillion in global sales at risk in 2026.
