# Support Ticket Process Map

## Current State

1. Customer submits a support ticket.
2. Ticket is assigned to a department.
3. Agent reviews and responds.
4. Ticket may be resolved, remain open, or escalate.
5. Manager reviews escalations after the fact.

## Pain Points

- SLA breaches are detected late.
- Negative customer sentiment is not prioritized.
- Agents may be overloaded.
- Root causes are not always visible.
- Managers may not know which tickets are likely to escalate.

## Future State

1. Customer submits a ticket.
2. System calculates SLA status, sentiment, and escalation risk.
3. High-risk tickets are prioritized.
4. Managers monitor SLA and workload dashboards.
5. Root-cause issues are addressed through process improvements.
